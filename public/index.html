Dreamy, ambient, psychedelic.

A music label and audio-tool maker for dreamers. The music is ambient, meditative, hypnotic or sharply psychedelic; the tools bend and spread sound into dreamy ambiences and dubby hypnotism. Born at the crossroads of immigrant experience and nomadic artistry, Nomad Resonance connects the mystical and the modern — a pilgrimage where tradition meets technology.
